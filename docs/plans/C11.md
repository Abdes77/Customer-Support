# C11 plan: Email

Goal: a business connects its inbox (Gmail by OAuth, Microsoft 365 / Outlook by OAuth, or any
IMAP/SMTP inbox on its own domain) and customers who write to it get the same bot as on every
other channel, answered in the same thread as a proper email with the business's signature,
while newsletters, automatic replies, bounces, internal mail and mail loops never get an
answer and the inbox never sends more than its limits allow.

Vendor docs read on 2026-11-12 (reachable from the VM): Gmail push notifications, the Gmail
synchronization guide and `users.history.list`; Cloud Pub/Sub push authentication; Microsoft
Graph `sendMail` (MIME), change notifications through webhooks (validation handshake,
`clientState`, lifecycle), `message: delta`. There are no Google, Microsoft or mailbox
credentials in the VM: the live checks go to `docs/HUMAN_TODO.md`.

## Pieces

1. **`@repo/channel-email` (stateless, no database):**
   - `mime/`: parse a raw RFC 5322 message (`postal-mime`) into `ParsedEmail` (ids, threading
     headers, addresses, text, HTML, attachments, all headers); build one (`nodemailer`'s
     `MailComposer`) with our Message-ID, In-Reply-To, References, a loop marker header and
     text plus HTML parts.
   - `body/`: the readable new text of a message: HTML to text when there is no text part,
     quoted replies cut (`On … wrote:`, Outlook's `From:/Sent:` block, `-----Original
     Message-----`, `>` lines), signatures cut (`-- `, "Sent from my iPhone" and similar).
   - `classify.ts`: bounce (DSN, `MAILER-DAEMON`, `X-Failed-Recipients`; the original
     Message-ID is read from the report), automatic reply (RFC 3834 `Auto-Submitted`,
     `X-Autoreply`, `Precedence: auto_reply`, out-of-office subjects), bulk (`List-Id`,
     `List-Unsubscribe`, `Precedence: bulk|list|junk`, no-reply senders), loop (our marker
     header), written by the business (the inbox, internal domains, its own domain unless it
     is a public mail service).
   - `thread.ts`: normalized Message-IDs, the thread key of a message (provider thread when
     there is one, else the root of References).
   - `compose.ts`: the outgoing email: greeting per `channels.email.greeting`, the engine's
     sign-off replaced by `channels.email.sign_off` and the signature, the customer's message
     quoted per `channels.email.quote_original`, HTML that reads hand-written.
   - Providers with an injected `fetch`: Gmail REST (profile, watch, history, raw messages,
     send), Microsoft Graph (me, delta, MIME content, MIME `sendMail`, subscriptions), Google
     and Microsoft OAuth (authorization URL with PKCE, code exchange, refresh); IMAP
     (`imapflow`) and SMTP (`nodemailer`), each connected to an address that was resolved and
     checked once (private, loopback and link-local refused, the address pinned, TLS checked
     against the host name).
   - `createEmailAdapter`: `send` builds the MIME message and sends it by the connection's
     provider; the result's id is our Message-ID (Graph returns none, and replies refer to
     it). `verifyWebhook`/`parseWebhook` refuse: email has its own notification routes.
2. **`/server` (database):** connection service for C16 (Google and Microsoft OAuth start and
   finish with a signed, cookie-bound state; IMAP/SMTP connect that tests both logins; list;
   disconnect), token refresh under the secret row lock, the Gmail push and Graph
   notification handlers (each notification matched to one connection by its unique key:
   the mailbox address for Gmail, the subscription id for Graph; Pub/Sub's OIDC token
   verified, Graph's `clientState` compared in constant time, the validation handshake
   answered), the sync job (`email-sync`, one run at a time per inbox: fetch new messages,
   classify, resolve the thread, store attachments, `ingest`), the crons (`email-poll` every
   minute: IMAP inboxes, and a safety sync of Gmail and Graph inboxes; `email-watch-renew`:
   Gmail watches and Graph subscriptions before expiry), the composer the send step calls,
   the per-inbox send throttle, and the channel guard.
3. **Pipeline (extension points, the turn flow unchanged):** `outboundComposers` (the send
   step's `prepare` lets the channel turn the stored text into the email it sends),
   `sendThrottles` (a send waits, with retry-after, when the inbox reached its hourly or daily
   limit), and `human_agent_replied.externalThreadId` (a person's reply from the mailbox lands
   in its own thread).
4. **Loop protection on what exists:** a marker header on every email we send (our own mail
   coming back is dropped; another instance's mail is never answered), automatic mail stored
   only inside an existing conversation and then left alone by the channel guard,
   `channels.email.max_replies_per_thread_per_day`, `conversation.max_bot_replies`, and the
   pipeline's send journal.
5. **A person in the mailbox:** mail the business itself writes in a customer thread (the Sent
   folder of Gmail, Outlook and IMAP, or a colleague's reply-all) becomes
   `human_agent_replied` and the provider-inbox takeover; `channels.email.skip_staffed_threads`
   keeps the bot out of threads the team is in.
6. **apps/web:** the adapter, guard, composer, throttle and jobs registered when email is
   available; `POST /api/webhooks/email/gmail`, `POST /api/webhooks/email/microsoft`; OAuth
   start/callback routes for Google and Microsoft; `POST /api/channels/email/imap`;
   `getEmail()` for C16.

## Tests (`pnpm verify`)

Fixture tests for parsing, cleaning, classification (newsletters, auto-replies in their usual
forms, bounces with the original id, internal mail, loops), threading and composition; Gmail,
Graph and IMAP threads end to end against fake APIs and an in-process IMAP server (`imapkit`)
plus SMTP server (`smtp-server`): a customer email in, one threaded reply out (In-Reply-To and
References on the reply, the same conversation on the next message), auto-replies and bounces
ignored, a bounce marking our message failed, a person's reply from the mailbox handing over;
the push routes (bad OIDC token, wrong clientState, handshake, another connection's id); the
SSRF refusal of private hosts; the send throttle; the IMAP/SMTP round trip through the real
routes, jobs and offline engine.
