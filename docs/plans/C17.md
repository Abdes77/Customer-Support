## Build order

1. Deployment mode (core runtime) and readiness check.
2. Limits guard (`packages/pipeline/src/limits`): rate limits and spend caps from `limits.*`,
   emergency exemption, a code-written notice to the customer once a day, in-app alert,
   `handoff` mode starts a takeover. New settings: `limits.notice_message`.
3. Tenancy fix (migration 0015), pending uploads table.
4. Retention job (`retention-purge`, daily) driven by `data.*`.
5. `@repo/observability`: Sentry envelope client with a scrubber, a fake transport, the
   logger hook; Next.js `instrumentation.ts`; trace explainer; per-address limiter; readiness.
6. Health routes, stuck-work alerts in the sweeper.
7. Findings N2–N15.
8. docs/THREAT_MODEL.md, docs/BACKUP_RESTORE.md, ARCHITECTURE, DECISIONS, HUMAN_TODO, SETTINGS;
   `pnpm verify`, `pnpm eval --mode mock`, `pnpm e2e`.
