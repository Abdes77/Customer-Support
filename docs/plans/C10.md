# C10 plan: Instagram and Messenger

Goal: a business connects its Facebook Page (and the Instagram professional account linked to
it) through Facebook Login for Business, and its customers talk to the same bot on Messenger
and Instagram Direct as on every other channel, with each platform's messaging window
enforced, the HUMAN_AGENT tag on a person's late replies, and the bot stepping back when a
person answers from Meta's own inbox.

Meta's docs were reachable (developers.facebook.com/documentation/...`.md`, read 2026-11-12):
Messenger webhooks and the event references (messages, message_echoes, message_deliveries,
message_reads, message_reactions, messaging_postbacks), Send a message (standard window,
messaging types, message tags), quick replies, sender actions, rate limits, the error-code
table; Instagram messaging webhooks (`object: "instagram"`, echoes inside `messages`,
`messaging_seen`, story replies and mentions), Instagram sending, quick replies, attachment
upload; Facebook Login for Business. Examples use Graph API v27.0 (C9's default).

## Pieces

1. **`@repo/channel-meta` (extended, not forked):** `GraphApiError.subcode`;
   `classifyGraphError` reads the subcode (10/2534022 and 10/2018278 → window closed;
   10/2018300 and 100/2534037 → another app controls the thread; 551, 200/1545041,
   10/2018108 → the person cannot be reached; none of them an auth failure); media
   downloads without a token (Messenger and Instagram attachment URLs are signed CDN links);
   `uploadMessageAttachment` (`POST /{page-id}/message_attachments`, multipart, `platform:
   instagram` for Instagram); `exchangeForLongLivedToken`.
2. **Contracts (additive):** `human_agent_replied.attribution` (`external`: the provider says
   who sent it; `unverified`: an echo that may be our own send); a `delivery_watermark`
   signal (Messenger reports deliveries and reads as "everything before this time");
   `SendTarget.standardWindowOpen` (the window without the human-agent extension, so the
   adapter knows when a person's reply needs the tag).
3. **`@repo/channel-messenger-instagram`** (stateless adapters, one factory per channel):
   parse `page` and `instagram` webhooks (`messaging` and `standby`): text, quick-reply taps,
   postbacks, attachments (image, video, audio, file, sticker, fallback/share links, reels,
   posts, story mentions), story replies (`kind: story_reply`), reactions, inline replies,
   referrals as metadata; deleted, ephemeral and admin-only notices skipped; echoes →
   `human_agent_replied` unless the echo is ours (our app id, or our metadata marker);
   deliveries (`mids` → statuses, watermark → signal), reads (Messenger watermark,
   Instagram `read.mid`), policy enforcement → `provider_event`. Send: text with
   `messaging_type: RESPONSE`, or `MESSAGE_TAG` + `HUMAN_AGENT` for a person's reply after the
   standard window; quick replies (≤13 × 20 characters, else written under the text); media
   uploaded from our storage then sent by attachment id; a metadata marker on every send.
   `markRead` (`mark_seen`), `sendTyping` (`typing_on`/`typing_off`), `fetchAttachment`
   (Meta hosts only, no token). Fixtures and a fake Graph API under `/testing`.
4. **`/server`:** Facebook Login completion (code → token → long-lived where it expires →
   the Pages the user granted → the chosen Page must be in that list with a messaging task
   → subscribe the Page's webhook fields → store the Messenger connection and, when linked
   and asked for, the Instagram one, each with the Page token encrypted; a Page or account
   of another business is refused before Meta is called), list and disconnect for C16, the
   provider-event handler (policy block/unblock, only on the named connection, never from
   before `connected_at`), and the story-reply guard (`channels.instagram.story_replies`).
5. **Pipeline:** the human-reply signal is matched against our own sends (stored ids,
   `message_parts`, the send journal; an `unverified` echo while one of our messages is being
   sent counts as ours when its text is part of that message); `delivery_watermark` applies
   statuses to the parts sent before it; the send step passes `standardWindowOpen`;
   `channelGuards` registered by the app next to the domain guards.
6. **apps/web:** both adapters, the event handler and the guard registered when
   `META_APP_SECRET` and `META_WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN` are set (webhook URLs
   `/api/webhooks/messenger` and `/api/webhooks/instagram`); `POST /api/oauth/meta/facebook`
   (agency staff, same-origin JSON); `getMetaPages()` for C16.

## Tests (`pnpm verify`)

Parser fixtures for every inbound kind on both platforms, echoes (ours / Page inbox /
unattributed), statuses and watermarks; send bodies (RESPONSE, HUMAN_AGENT, quick replies,
media upload, typing, mark_seen); error classification; Facebook Login against a fake Graph
(including another business's Page refused); pipeline: echo attribution and the send race,
watermarks; apps/web, through the real route, jobs, offline engine and fake Graph: round trip
on Messenger and Instagram, duplicate delivery stored and answered once, a Page-inbox reply
hands over and the bot stays silent, our own echo does not, window rules (bot refused after
24 h; a person's reply tagged HUMAN_AGENT between 24 h and 7 days, refused after), a story
reply ignored per setting, a bad signature never stored.
