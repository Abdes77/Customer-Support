# C12 plan: Scheduling

Goal: a lead whose routing says "book a meeting" is offered two or three real times that fit
the business's settings and its connected calendar, books the one they pick (the event
carries their details and the lead report), and can confirm, move or cancel it in the same
conversation; reminders go out before the meeting. Calendly is the alternative: the bot sends
a single-use link and the booking is recorded from Calendly's webhook. Every piece is
switchable per business through settings.

Vendor docs read on 2026-11-12 (reachable from the VM): Google Calendar `events`
(resource, `list`, `insert`: client-chosen base32hex ids, `transparency`, `eventType`,
attendee `responseStatus`), Microsoft Graph `event` (`showAs`, `isAllDay`, `transactionId`),
`calendarView` and `POST /events`; Calendly "Create Single-Use Scheduling Link", "Create
Webhook Subscription", "Webhook Signatures", the Invitee resource (`tracking.utm_*`,
`rescheduled`, `old_invitee`) and the rescheduling payload guide. No Google, Microsoft or
Calendly credentials exist on the VM: live checks go to `docs/HUMAN_TODO.md`.

## Pieces

1. **Contract and prompt (additive, C4's files):** a `cancel_meeting` action and tool
   (`reason`); `book_meeting` moves an existing meeting (rescheduling); the business block
   gets a "Meetings" part (confirmation guidance, rescheduling and cancelling rules) and the
   Calendly host joins the allowed link domains when Calendly is the provider. Render
   version r3. Dry-run runtime and the eval sandbox learn the new action.
2. **Settings:** `scheduling.tentative_events`, `scheduling.all_day_events`,
   `scheduling.reminder_message`, `scheduling.reminder_confirm_message`,
   `scheduling.unbooked_alert_hours`.
3. **Database (migration 0011):** at most one active (pending or confirmed) booking per
   conversation; a unique Calendly webhook key per integration connection;
   `directory.findIntegrationConnectionByWebhookKey`.
4. **`@repo/scheduling`:**
   - `availability/`: pure slot computation in the business's time zone (wall-clock slots
     built per local date, so daylight-saving days are right; non-existent local times are
     skipped), business or custom hours, federal and own holidays, minimum lead time,
     maximum days ahead, buffers (plus a travel rule's extra time), meetings per day, the
     arrival window, busy calendar events (tentative and all-day per settings, declined and
     free events ignored), the customer's preference in words, spreading the offers, labels
     in the customer's time zone.
   - `calendars/`: Google Calendar and Microsoft Graph clients (injected `fetch`): list
     calendars, busy events, create (idempotent: Google event id derived from the booking
     id, Graph `transactionId`), move, cancel.
   - `calendly/`: client (current user, event types, single-use links with a tracking token,
     webhook subscriptions, cancel), signature check (`t=…,v1=…`, constant time, five-minute
     tolerance), payload parsing.
   - `oauth/`: PKCE, signed state bound to a cookie (own purpose label), authorization URLs
     and token exchange/refresh for Google (calendar scopes), Microsoft (`Calendars.ReadWrite`)
     and Calendly.
   - `server/`: connection service (availability, start/complete OAuth, Calendly by token,
     list with masked metadata, calendars to check and to book into, disconnect); access
     tokens refreshed under the secret's row lock; the booking service (offer, book,
     reschedule, cancel) with a per-business advisory lock, the calendar checked again at
     booking time and stale holds released; action handlers; the Calendly webhook handler;
     jobs `scheduling-reminders` (event `scheduling.booking_changed`, versioned so a moved or
     cancelled meeting's old reminders stop) and `scheduling-meeting-requested` (subscriber to
     `lead.meeting_requested`: an in-app alert when the team has to book by hand, or when no
     meeting was booked after `scheduling.unbooked_alert_hours`).
   - `/testing`: fake Google Calendar, Graph and Calendly APIs and fixtures.
5. **Pipeline:** handlers registered in the domain composition (with optional injected
   scheduling dependencies); `enqueueBotMessage` for code-written messages such as reminders.
6. **apps/web:** `POST /api/oauth/calendar/{google|microsoft|calendly}` and its callback,
   `POST /api/integrations/calendly/token`, `POST /api/webhooks/calendly/{key}`, the jobs,
   `getScheduling()` for C16.
7. **Docs:** ARCHITECTURE "As built (C12)", BUILD_PLAN delivered line, DECISIONS, HUMAN_TODO
   (live checks), SETTINGS regenerated, CLAUDE.md.

## Safety rules

- Book only a lead whose current routing is `book_meeting` (contact-later, disqualified,
  handed off and unverified-location leads are never booked directly), with the details
  `routing.booking_requires` names.
- Re-run tool calls and retried turns never book twice: one active booking per conversation
  (unique index), same slot again = the same booking, vendor create calls are idempotent.
- Two customers cannot take one slot: holds are written under a per-business advisory lock
  and checked against every active booking (buffers included) and the calendar again.
- Customer text in a calendar event is plain data: one line per value, angle brackets
  neutralized, length-capped; Graph bodies are sent as text.
- Tokens never leave the server: encrypted, never logged or put in a job payload.
