# C13 plan: handoff, alerts, emergencies and follow-ups

Owner paths: `packages/handoff`, the Twilio and acknowledgement routes in `apps/web`. Shared files
touched the documented way: events in `packages/core/src/events`, a migration and directory
lookups in `packages/db`, registrations in `packages/pipeline/src/composition`, wiring in
`apps/web/src/server`, env spec, docs.

## What exists and what is missing

- Settings for alerts, emergencies, takeover, follow-ups and quiet hours exist (C2); nothing reads
  most of them. Tables exist: `alerts`, `escalations`, `takeover_locks`, `scheduled_jobs`,
  `contacts.opted_out_at`.
- The pipeline guards takeovers and opt-outs (claim and send), starts a `rule` takeover on a
  refusal and a `provider_inbox` takeover on a reply from the provider's inbox. It runs turns only
  for `message.received`: there is no way to start a `follow_up` or `takeover_released` turn.
- Nothing detects an opt-out keyword. Nothing releases a takeover. `handoff_to_human`,
  `escalate_emergency` and `schedule_follow_up` are recorded as `deferred`.
- `hours.quiet_hours_cover_replies` is declared and read nowhere (DECISIONS 98).

## Pipeline extension points (additive; the turn flow and send flow stay as they are)

1. `conversation.turn_requested` (event, ids only, trigger `follow_up` | `takeover_released`) and
   the job `pipeline-requested-turn`: the same claim (lease, guards with the turn's trigger),
   generate and commit steps as a reply, with no pending messages; it gives way when unanswered
   customer messages exist (the reply run answers them), and a newer customer message always
   supersedes its drafts.
2. `InboundObserver` (`PipelineDeps.inboundObservers`, domain composition): runs inside the
   transaction that stores a new inbound message, under a savepoint, and returns events to emit
   with `message.received`. C13 uses it for opt-out and opt-in keywords, for stopping follow-ups
   on a reply and for takeover activity. Deterministic: the opt-out is recorded before the reply
   turn can start.
3. `SendGate` (`PipelineDeps.sendGates`, domain composition): asked by the send job after the
   claim; may hold a bot message until a time (the job sleeps; a held message does not block the
   messages after it) or withhold it. C13's quiet-hours gate: replies during quiet hours when
   `hours.quiet_hours_cover_replies` is on (never a turn that escalated an emergency), and
   unprompted messages (follow-up turns) always; a held nudge the customer has answered since is
   withheld.
4. `prepare`: a takeover lock may exempt the bot messages up to a sequence
   (`takeover_locks.exempt_through_seq`), so the bot's own handoff message goes out; a message
   with purpose `opt_out_confirmation` passes the opt-out check (not the takeover check).
5. `enqueueHumanMessage` next to `enqueueBotMessage` (manual replies from the admin panel).

## packages/handoff

- **Alerts** (`src/alerts`): recipients from `alerts.recipients` + `alerts.agency_recipients`,
  per-recipient channels and events, `alerts.events`, `alerts.minimum_score`, `alerts.detail`,
  `alerts.include_contact_details`, `alerts.hold_overnight`. Text built by code from the newest
  lead report, customer-written values flattened to one inert line (no markup, no live links),
  link `APP_URL/inbox/<conversation id>` (sign-in required). One alert row per recipient and
  channel (dedupe key); one "decision" alert per lead and recipient across `lead.qualified`,
  `lead.meeting_requested` and the routing `lead.handoff`. Transports behind ports: Twilio SMS
  and voice (REST, injected `fetch`, form-encoded, basic auth), SMTP email (nodemailer, plain
  text), WhatsApp through the agency's Cloud API number with an approved template. A failed
  delivery is recorded and the job goes on to the next recipient.
- **Emergency chain** (`src/emergency`): `escalate_emergency` handler writes the escalation
  (one active per conversation, chain snapshot and policy from settings) and emits
  `handoff.escalation_started`; `lead.emergency` starts it too when the handler could not.
  `handoff-emergency-chain`: for each round, for each contact: alert by their channels → wait
  `emergency.acknowledge_minutes` → call (Twilio voice, press 1) when
  `emergency.call_unacknowledged` → short wait → next; after `emergency.rounds` the agency's
  fallback contacts; then `exhausted` with a critical in-app alert. Every delivery is one alert
  row (dedupe per step and channel), sent while holding the escalation row lock, so nothing is
  sent after an acknowledgement commits and a restarted run never alerts a step twice. Failures
  are recorded and the chain moves on. Acknowledgement: link (GET shows a page, POST
  acknowledges; 256-bit token per alert row, hashed), SMS reply with the alert's 6-digit code
  from the alerted number, keypress 1 on the call, or a signed-in user; conditional update
  `active → acknowledged` once, with who, how and when.
- **Takeover** (`src/takeover`): `handoff_to_human` handler + turn observer (the lock is
  created at commit, exempting the turn's own messages); `createHandoffService` for C16: start,
  release, state, manual reply, opt-out on/off, acknowledge, list escalations, test alert;
  `handoff-takeover-release`: sleeps until last activity + `handoff.auto_release_minutes`,
  re-reads, releases (`idle_timeout`) and, with `handoff.on_release` = check-in, requests a
  `takeover_released` turn.
- **Follow-ups** (`src/follow-ups`): planned at commit by a turn observer after a bot reply (one
  active plan per conversation, a `scheduled_jobs` row, nudge number), or by
  `schedule_follow_up`; `handoff-follow-up` sleeps until due, re-checks (customer replied by
  sequence, opt-out, takeover, booking, outcome, ended, max nudges, window), moves the time out
  of quiet hours (customer's zone) and, per `follow_up.send_window` and the after-hours mode,
  into business hours, then emits `conversation.turn_requested`. The engine writes the nudge.
- **Opt-out** (`src/opt-out.ts`): keyword match on a short message, record (time, source,
  channel, message, keyword) on the contact and in the audit log, cancel planned follow-ups,
  one confirmation; opt-in lifts it. Also a turn guard as a backstop.
- **Time** (`src/time.ts`): quiet hours, next allowed moment, the customer's time zone (contact,
  else area code per `hours.customer_time_zone`, else the business).
- **Jobs**: `handoff-alerts-*` (one per `lead.*` event and `message.send_failed`),
  `handoff-emergency-start` (`lead.emergency`), `handoff-emergency-chain`,
  `handoff-takeover-release`, `handoff-follow-up`, `handoff-sweep` (cron: lost follow-up,
  chain and release events).
- **Twilio webhooks** (`src/twilio`): signature check (HMAC-SHA1 over URL + sorted params,
  constant time), inbound SMS acknowledgement, voice gather, status callbacks.
