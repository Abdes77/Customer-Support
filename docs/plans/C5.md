# C5 plan: lead scoring, reports and routing

Goal: `packages/leads` keeps one lead per conversation up to date after every turn: each
qualification criterion with its value, confidence and evidence; a score from the criterion
weights in settings; a written report; and a routing decision from the score bands and the
routing settings. The decision goes back to the model as the result of `update_lead_fields`,
so the reply of the same turn follows it, and the five `lead.*` events go out for C12 to C16.

## Pure core (no I/O, `src/assess`)

1. **Criteria** (`criteria.ts`). One evaluation per enabled criterion and custom criterion:
   state (`known`, `declined` = recorded as null, `unconfirmed` = below
   `scoring.minimum_confidence`, `missing`), the credit it earns (0 to 1) and, where a fit
   rule applies, the fit action of the settings (`lower_score`, `contact_later`,
   `disqualify`). Rules per criterion: service offered or turned away
   (`service_type.*`), kind of job and smallest job (`job_scope.*`, the stated budget stands
   for the job size), service area (`service_area.ts`), ideal and acceptable timelines,
   decision-maker roles, budget bands and minimum, property types, insurance and financing
   preferences, photos, custom criteria with ideal answers.
2. **Service area** (`service-area.ts`). Excluded ZIP, state, listed ZIP, listed place;
   nothing configured accepts everything. A radius cannot be checked without a ZIP
   geography table: the location is then `unverified` (team confirms, no direct booking).
3. **Score** (`score.ts`). `100 × Σ weight × credit / Σ weight`, unknown criteria earn
   `scoring.unknown_credit_percent`, then the `scoring.adjustments` for the signals seen
   (the six the model records, `emergency`, `shared_phone`, `sent_photos`), clamped to 0 to
   100 and rounded. Band from `scoring.thresholds`.
4. **Routing** (`route.ts`). In order: a not-a-fit category in `scoring.auto_disqualify` →
   disqualify; a fit rule that says disqualify → disqualify; a required criterion still
   missing or unconfirmed → no decision yet (keep qualifying); otherwise the band's outcome
   (`routing.mode`, `routing.<band>_outcome`), made no friendlier than any fit rule
   (contact_later), the existing-customer rule, a required criterion the customer would not
   answer, or an unverified location (all three: not booked directly). Reasons are written
   in code from labels only, never with customer text.
5. **Status** from the decision: qualifying, qualified (contact later), meeting_requested,
   disqualified; `meeting_booked` and `handed_off` belong to C12/C13 and are kept.
6. **Report** (`report.ts`). Sections who, job, where, urgency, budget signals, fit, red
   flags, next step, plus a one-line summary and Markdown. Customer-written values are
   quoted as data (one line, length-capped).

## Storage and wiring

7. **Migration 0007.** `lead_events` (outbox: one row per event with a per-lead
   `dedupe_key`, `emitted_at`) and `routing_decisions.sequence` (order within a lead; two
   decisions in one transaction share `created_at`, DECISIONS 60).
8. **Handlers** (`update_lead_fields`, `mark_not_a_fit`): one transaction that creates the
   lead if needed, merges the fields (a guess never overwrites a stated answer), assesses,
   stores a new routing decision and report version when they changed, updates the lead
   row and writes outbox rows for the transitions. Dry-run mode reads and writes nothing.
9. **Turn observer** (new pipeline extension point, called inside the commit transaction
   under a savepoint): makes sure every conversation that had a turn has a lead with a
   score and a report, records `escalate_emergency` (lead.emergency), `handoff_to_human` and
   refusals (lead.handoff), and returns the lead's unemitted events, which the emit step
   sends with the turn's own events and then marks.
10. **Outbox cron** `leads-outbox` (every minute): sends what a failed or crashed turn left
    behind. Event ids are the row ids, so a second send is dropped by Inngest.
11. **Lead loader** orders decisions by `sequence`. `leadRouting` for dry runs
    (`createDryRunConversation({ routing })`) uses the same assessment.

## Tests

- 14 leads across roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodeling and pest control, under
  three routing configurations (defaults, always "we'll contact you", a strict profile with
  a service area and cold leads disqualified): exact score, band, outcome, status, reasons
  and report sections.
- Unit tests for each criterion rule, the service area, adjustments and the merge rule.
- Database tests: handler round trip, idempotence, events emitted once, observer, outbox
  cron, isolation matrix entry; a pipeline turn with a scripted engine that calls
  `update_lead_fields` gets the decision in the tool result.
