# Threat model

Written by C17 (security and reliability) and revised by the independent audit that followed
it (rows marked **Audit**; its summary is section 6). For every
threat: what an attacker (or an accident) could do, the mitigation, the file that implements
it and the test that proves it. Accepted risks are listed separately (section 4) with the
reason each is accepted; checks that need a vendor account are in section 5. Paths are
repository-relative; a test is named as `file › test name`.

Read with `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §3 (tenancy and security), the "As built" sections, and
`docs/DECISIONS.md` (the numbers below are its entries).

People management (granting and removing access, section 2.12) was added in C18, **after** the
independent audit of section 6: it has been covered by C18's own review and tests only, not by
an independent audit.

## 1. Assets, actors, trust boundaries

- **Assets.** Customers' messages, photos, contact details and lead reports; businesses'
  settings, knowledge and playbooks; tenant credentials (OAuth tokens, page tokens, IMAP/SMTP
  passwords, CRM keys, webhook signing secrets); platform secrets (env); the model budget;
  the emergency escalation path (a person must hear of a gas leak).
- **Actors.** Customers (untrusted text, photos, emails on every channel); anyone on the
  internet (webhook routes, the widget API, acknowledgement links); vendors' callbacks
  (Meta, Google, Microsoft, Twilio, Calendly); agency staff (trusted within their agency,
  can reach Supabase's REST API with their own token); client users (read-only, one business);
  operators (environment, deployment).
- **Boundaries.** (1) Public HTTP → `apps/web` routes (signature, token or origin checks);
  (2) routes and jobs → PostgreSQL (row-level security, one tenant per transaction);
  (3) customer text → the model (data blocks, narrow validated tools, output guard);
  (4) our process → vendors and user-supplied hosts (pinned, checked addresses);
  (5) our process → logs and Sentry (redaction and scrubbing).
