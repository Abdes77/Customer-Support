## What was built

- `packages/handoff` (new): alerts (recipients, kinds, dedupe per lead decision, hold
  overnight, plain customer text, conversation link only), transports behind a small
  interface (Twilio SMS and voice over the REST API, SMTP through nodemailer, the agency's
  WhatsApp Cloud API number with an approved template), the emergency escalation chain
  (snapshot, plan, steps, waits, calls, rounds, agency fallback, exhausted), acknowledgement
  by link page (GET shows, POST acknowledges), text-message code from the alerted number,
  keypress on the call, or a signed-in person; Twilio webhook handlers with signature checks;
  takeover (handoff action, idle release, manual release, manual replies); opt-out and opt-in
  words; follow-ups (cadence, maximum nudges, check-ins from `schedule_follow_up`, quiet
  hours in the customer's zone with a US area-code table, business hours and after-hours
  mode, channel windows); the quiet-hours send gate (implements
  `hours.quiet_hours_cover_replies`, DECISIONS 98 closed); jobs (`handoff-alert-*`,
  `handoff-emergency-start`, `handoff-emergency-chain`, `handoff-takeover-release`,
  `handoff-follow-up`, `handoff-sweep`); the admin service for C15/C16; `/testing` fakes;
  `pnpm --filter @repo/handoff live:alerts`.
- Pipeline (additive extension points, DECISIONS 209, 210): `conversation.turn_requested` and
  `pipeline-requested-turn` (follow-ups and check-ins are pipeline turns with trigger
  `follow_up` / `takeover_released`), `InboundObserver`s (inside the store transaction),
  `SendGate`s (holds don't block later messages), `takeover_locks.exempt_through_seq` and the
  `opt_out_confirmation` exception in `prepare`, `enqueueHumanMessage`; handoff registered in
  `createDomainComposition()`.
- Database: migration `0012_handoff.sql` (escalation policy, next action, who/how
  acknowledged, one active chain per conversation; alerts' escalation and acknowledgement
  hashes; takeover exemption), two directory lookups (`findAlert`,
  `findActiveEmergencyAlertsByRecipient`). Events `handoff.escalation_started`,
  `handoff.follow_up_planned`, `conversation.turn_requested`. Env `ALERT_WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE`,
  `ALERT_WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_LANGUAGE`.
- apps/web: handoff wired in `buildServerPipeline` (jobs, transports from env, service,
  Twilio handlers), `getHandoff()`, routes `POST /api/handoff/twilio/{sms|voice|status}` and
  `GET|POST /api/handoff/ack/{token}`, `/api/handoff` left out of the session proxy.
- Evals: the sandbox consults production's verdict for `escalate_emergency` and
  `schedule_follow_up` (settings only, nothing written).

### Nothing is sent during quiet hours

- "waits out the customer's quiet hours, and business hours when it has to": a nudge due at
  10 PM in the customer's zone (from the contact's area code) sleeps until 8:00 AM
  (`2026-11-11T14:00:00.000Z`) before the turn is asked for; with business hours only it
  waits for Thursday 8:00 (Wednesday is Veterans Day).
- Quiet-hours gate: "holds unprompted messages until quiet hours end and drops a nudge the
  customer overtook" (hold until 08:00, then `withhold: customer_replied`); "holds replies
  only when the business asks for it, and never an emergency's"
  (`hours.quiet_hours_cover_replies`).
- Send job: "lets a person's reply pass a bot message a gate holds, and sends the held one
  after" (the hold is a sleep; the person's reply is not kept behind it).
- Unit: quiet hours across midnight and on both daylight-saving nights, next allowed moment
  across zones, after-hours modes that require business hours, alert hold overnight.

### Takeover pauses the bot

- "keeps the bot silent while a person holds the conversation and resumes after the
  release" (turn skipped `takeover`, a person's reply goes out under the lock, the next
  message after the release is answered).
- "sends the bot's own handoff message and nothing after it" (`handoff_to_human`: the lock
  starts at commit with `exempt_through_seq`, the handoff message is delivered, a later bot
  message is withheld `takeover`, the customer's next message is not answered).
- "releases after the idle time, counting from the last message of either side" (customer
  message during the sleep moves the release from 18:00 to 19:00; release event and
  check-in turn request), "takes over, replies and releases from the admin panel", "keeps a
  takeover awake while the customer writes"; the existing Messenger hand-over test (I5) in
  `apps/web` passes with the release job running.

### The emergency chain escalates when an alert goes unacknowledged

- "alerts, calls and moves on through the chain and the agency when nobody acknowledges":
  Ann (text) → call Ann → Bob (WhatsApp, email) → call Bob → agency (text) → call → exhausted
  with a critical in-app alert; waits of 5 and 2 minutes on the injected clock; the stored
  rows hold neither the code nor the link secret.
- "stops at the first acknowledgement by text-message code, exactly once" (a stranger's
  number and a wrong code match nothing; the right code from Ann's number acknowledges; the
  chain stops; the record says who, how and when; one audit entry),
  "acknowledges through the link's page (POST only) and the keypress on the call",
  "records one acknowledgement when two arrive at once, whichever wins", "answers
  text-message replies through Twilio's signed webhook", "moves straight on when an alert
  cannot be sent, and repeats nothing when a step runs again", "starts one chain per
  conversation, none when emergencies are off, and flags a business with nobody to alert".
- Through the app (`apps/web/src/server/handoff/handoff.test.ts`): "puts safety first,
  alerts the chain and the recipients, calls, and ends the chain unanswered" (webhook →
  reply turn with the offline model → `escalate_emergency` → chain jobs → text and call to
  Ann, email to the alert recipient; the reply starts with the gas-leak instructions), the
  acknowledgement page and Twilio's signed status callback.
