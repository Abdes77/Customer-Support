## Decisions worth knowing (docs/DECISIONS.md 61 to 78)

- One definition per durable function, two runners (Inngest and in-process); the runner is
  picked from the environment, and a hosted deployment without Inngest refuses to emit.
- Correctness does not depend on Inngest's debounce or concurrency: the turn lease, the
  `handled_through_seq` watermark, sequence numbers and the predecessor check do the work.
- Guard stops move the watermark (a person's backlog is theirs); failures and supersedes do
  not. Bot messages are checked again for takeover at send time.
- Superseded drafts are kept, marked `superseded`.
- One stored message may leave as several provider messages; all part ids are recorded.
- Reactions start no turn; messages for revoked or disabled connections are acknowledged and
  not stored; an onboarding business answers only on the test channel.
