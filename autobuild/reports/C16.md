## What was built

- **Inbox** (`/inbox`, `/inbox/[id]`): filters (business, channel, open/closed, unread,
  assigned to me/unassigned, taken over), search over names, phone, email and message text
  (taken literally), live updates; conversation with attachments, lead panel (criteria with
  evidence, score, report, routing history, meetings), takeover, reply box, release, internal
  notes, assignment, emergencies with acknowledgement, follow-ups.
- **Manual reply exactly once:** `@repo/handoff` `takeOver` claims a takeover nobody holds and
  never takes one from a colleague; `sendManualReply({ requireHolder, idempotencyKey })`;
  `@repo/pipeline` `enqueueHumanMessage` stores the key and returns the first message for a
  repeated key under the conversation's row lock (tests in both packages).
- **Leads** (`/leads`) and the CSV (`GET /api/leads/export`): same filters, same
  authorization, formula neutralization, at most 5,000 rows, audited.
- **Analytics** (`/analytics`): conversations, first response, qualification rate, booked
  meetings, drop-off funnel, by business, channel and segment.
- **Channel and integration pages** on the services of C8 to C14, stored state only.
- **Audit log viewer** (`/audit`) for agency admins, values redacted.
- **Client portal permissions** per screen (`apps/web/src/server/portal.ts`), nav included.
- **Live updates:** Supabase Realtime `postgres_changes` when configured (migration 0014
  publishes the tables when `supabase_realtime` exists), polling otherwise.
- **Seed:** a website-chat connection per business and two website-chat conversations.
- Docs: ARCHITECTURE "As built (C16)", BUILD_PLAN (delivered note), DECISIONS 261 to 270,
  HUMAN_TODO 18, plan in docs/plans/C16.md.
