## Skipped or changed, and why

- **No model writes the report.** It is written by code after every change (always there,
  reproducible, cannot say more than the record holds). The unused `model.lead_report.*`
  settings were removed from the registry (DECISIONS 122).
- **Service radius is not checked** (no ZIP geography in the product): such locations are
  "unverified" and are not booked directly (DECISIONS 126, HUMAN_TODO 9).
- No live check is needed: lead scoring calls no vendor. With an API key, the live
  conversation checks of HUMAN_TODO 2 exercise it.
- Tests that pinned the pre-C5 behaviour were updated: `update_lead_fields` is now
  `applied` (pipeline composition test, web round trip), and a conversation has a lead after
  its first turn (reply test).
