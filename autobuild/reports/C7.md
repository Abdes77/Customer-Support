## What was built

`packages/evals` (about 13,000 lines with tests) and the command around it:

- **Scenarios**: 12 personas × 6 trades = 72, in six suites; five settings profiles and
  five channels by a fixed rotation; a 24-scenario `--core` selection. Six made-up
  businesses with their facts, their "not in the facts" lists and their jobs. The expected
  routing of each scenario is computed by `assessLead` from the customer's true facts.
- **Sandbox**: a throwaway PostgreSQL database with the production composition on top
  (engine, full-text knowledge retriever, playbook loader); lead state in the dry run's
  memory with the real scoring rules. After a run the tables a live turn writes are
  counted: all zero (invariant I8, tested).
- **Simulated customer** (`claude-opus-5-5`, low effort) and its rule-based stand-in;
  opening lines and all attack lines are fixed text.
- **Grading**: 26 code checks (13 for rules the engine enforces in code, 13 for behaviour
  that depends on the model), each with a test that makes it fail; a judge
  (`claude-opus-5-5`, high effort, one strict tool) for seven criteria and fifteen rules
  rendered from the settings registry; quotes verified against the transcript.
- **Runner**: suites side by side under one concurrency limit, retries with jittered
  backoff, a wall-clock ceiling per conversation, an errors sidecar, resume, cost from
  reported tokens.
- **Baseline** compare and save, **verdict** and exit codes, **HTML report** (one file, no
  script, light and dark), `summary.md`.
- **`pnpm eval --calibrate-judge`**: eight hand-made conversations with known faults for
  the judge (an invented visit time, a guarantee and a license number never stated, a price
  in words, a revealed score, a mishandled emergency), per the C4 review note that
  availability, licenses and warranties are guarded only by the prompt.
- Docs: `docs/plans/C7.md`, ARCHITECTURE "As built (C7)" and the testing table, DECISIONS
  129–143, HUMAN_TODO entry 10 and the "unverified" list, BUILD_PLAN (what later chunks
  keep green), CLAUDE.md (the command; 79 lines).

Tests (`packages/evals`, 160): scenarios and oracle 17, checks 31, customer simulator 14,
judge 17, scoring 9, summary, baseline and verdict 19, conversation loop against the
