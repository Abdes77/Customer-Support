## What was built

- **`packages/channels/widget`** (`@repo/channel-widget`, `/server`): the channel adapter (no
  webhooks; `send`/`sendTyping` poke the visitor's browser through a content-free Supabase
  Realtime broadcast, or do nothing when Realtime is not configured), signed visitor session
  tokens (HMAC-SHA256 with `WIDGET_SESSION_SECRET`, checked against the `widget_sessions` row on
  every call), allowed-origin matching, photo sniffing by magic bytes, fixed-window rate limits
  in `usage_counters`, and `createWidgetService` (config, session start/resume, pre-chat
  profile, message post with photo → `pipeline.ingest`, message list with cursor and typing
  flag). For C16: `widgetEmbedInfo` (snippet, key, status, allowed sites),
  `ensureWidgetConnection`, `rotateWidgetKey`, `setWidgetEnabled`.
- **`packages/widget-embed`**: the browser script (plain TypeScript in a shadow root: bubble,
  teaser, panel with logo/title/subtitle, closed-hours notice, greeting, messages with photos
  and quick replies, typing dots, pre-chat form, composer with photo button, privacy note;
  full screen under 640 px; session and open state in `localStorage`; Realtime client or
  polling), bundled by esbuild into the committed `src/bundle.generated.ts`
  (`pnpm --filter @repo/widget-embed build`): 17.2 KB raw, 7.0 KB gzipped (budget 12 KB).
- **`apps/web`**: `/api/widget/v1/{widget.js,config,session,profile,messages}` (thin routes;
  CORS, byte-capped bodies and error mapping in `src/server/widget`); the adapter and the
  service registered in `src/server/pipeline` (polling without Supabase, Realtime with it; on a
  hosted deployment without `WIDGET_SESSION_SECRET` the widget is off and the API answers 503).
- **Shared changes**: contract `packages/core/src/contracts/widget.ts` (new, owner C8);
  migration `0008_widget_session_profile` (`widget_sessions.profile`, `last_message_at`);
  `TenantStorage.putUpload` / `uploadPath` in `@repo/db/storage`; setting
  `limits.widget_messages_per_business_per_minute` (SETTINGS.md regenerated); boundary edge
  `@repo/channel-widget` → `@repo/ai` (hours); catalog `esbuild ^0.28.2`; Playwright setup
  (`apps/web/playwright.config.ts`, `apps/web/e2e`, `pnpm e2e`, `.github/workflows/e2e.yml`).
