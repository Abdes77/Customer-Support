# T1 · slugify tests (tests only)

## Outcome

`test/slugify.test.js` pins down the behaviour of a function `slugify(text)` that does not exist
yet. The next step implements it and may not change this file, so the tests are the
specification. They are expected to fail until then.

## Read

- package.json (module type and test script)
- test/smoke.test.js (the test style to follow)

## Owns

- test/slugify.test.js

## Interfaces

- The tests import `{ slugify }` from `../src/slugify.js`.

## Done when

- `node --check test/slugify.test.js` exits 0
- there is one test for each of these:
  - "Hello World" gives "hello-world"
  - accents are removed: "Crème Brûlée" gives "creme-brulee"
  - runs of spaces, punctuation and underscores become one hyphen: "a  b__c -- d!" gives "a-b-c-d"
  - leading and trailing separators are dropped: "  --Hi--  " gives "hi"
  - an empty string, or one with no letters or digits, gives ""
  - a non-string argument throws a TypeError

## Out of scope

- src/slugify.js. Do not create it; failing tests are the expected state after this step.
